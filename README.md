# PRIVACY POLICY

**Effective Date:** 19 October 2026

**Last Updated:** 19 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Deepak Phenolics Limited** ("we", "our", "us"), part of the Deepak Group, collects, uses and protects personal information when we communicate with customers, employees and other contacts through WhatsApp. We use the WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) provided by Meta, and our internal systems, including n8n workflow automation, ERP/SAP and CRM.

By using our WhatsApp services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Who this policy applies to

- **Customers** who receive order, delivery order and dispatch updates from us.
- **Employees** who receive work notifications such as visit reminders, alerts and reports.
- **Anyone** who sends us a message on WhatsApp.

## 1A. Purpose and scope of our WhatsApp use

We use WhatsApp only for business-to-business and internal operational communication: order confirmation, delivery order and dispatch notifications to our existing business customers, and work notifications to our employees. We do not sell products or services through WhatsApp, we do not run a product catalogue, shopping cart or payment on WhatsApp, and we do not send advertising or unsolicited promotional messages to the general public. We comply with the WhatsApp Business Terms of Service and WhatsApp Business policies.

## 2. Information we collect and use

**Information from our business systems (ERP/SAP, CRM)**

- Customer name, company name, mobile number and WhatsApp number.
- Order and dispatch details: sales order, delivery order and purchase order numbers, product, quantity, dispatch date, transporter and vehicle details.
- For employees: name, mobile number, visit schedules and meeting notes needed for work.

This information is used in the WhatsApp messages we send you, such as order and dispatch updates. We do not collect it directly from you on WhatsApp.

**Information you send to us on WhatsApp**

- Your WhatsApp number and profile name.
- Messages you send to us, and message details such as timestamps and message IDs.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data unless you choose to provide it in a conversation.

## 3. How and why we use your information

- To send transactional updates such as order confirmations, delivery order and dispatch notifications.
- To send work notifications to employees, such as visit reminders, alerts and reports.
- To answer enquiries, register complaints and provide customer support.
- To maintain business records, analyse and improve our services and workflows.
- To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

We use your information only for business communication and service-related purposes. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data.

## 4. Consent and opt-out

We send WhatsApp messages from Deepak Phenolics Limited only to customers and employees who have agreed to receive them, for example when they provide their WhatsApp number to us during onboarding or while placing an order, with a clear statement that they will receive order and dispatch updates from Deepak Phenolics Limited on WhatsApp.

You can stop receiving messages at any time by replying **STOP** to any message or by emailing dnsharma@godeepak.com. We stop sending messages promptly after your request.

## 5. WhatsApp and Meta platform

Our messages are delivered through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform and are subject to Meta's own terms and privacy policy. Meta may process data on servers outside India.

## 6. Who we share data with

- Meta Platforms, Inc., to deliver messages.
- Our internal and hosting systems (n8n, ERP/SAP, CRM and databases), which are operated by or for Deepak.
- Government or regulatory authorities, where required by law.

## 7. Data storage and retention

We keep message logs and related business records for up to **24 months** and then delete or anonymise them, unless a longer period is required by law. When we receive a valid deletion request, we act on it within **30 days**.

## 8. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including secure servers, encrypted connections, restricted access to internal systems and regular monitoring of our automation workflows. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

## 9. Your rights

Under applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the right to:

- Request access to your personal data
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Withdraw your consent and opt out of future communications
- Raise a complaint about how your data is handled

To exercise these rights, email dnsharma@godeepak.com.
